# vocab
luggage|my + luggage + be + adjective|Luggage est indénombrable : pas de « s ». On dit « some luggage » ou « a piece of luggage ». My luggage is very heavy.
departure|the + departure + time|Departure est un nom : « the departure time » = l'heure de départ. The departure time is nine o'clock.
delay|there is + a + duration + delay|« There is a delay » annonce un retard. On place la durée avant delay : a two-hour delay.
journey|it was + a + adjective + journey|Journey est le trajet en lui-même. On le décrit avec un adjectif : a long journey.
accommodation|book + accommodation|Accommodation est indénombrable en anglais britannique : we booked our accommodation, en anglais britannique, pas de « s ».
itinerary|our + itinerary + include + noun|Itinerary est l'itinéraire prévu. Le verbe include introduit ce qu'il contient : Our itinerary includes three cities.
passport|don't forget + your + passport|À l'impératif négatif : Don't + verbe. Passport prend « your » : Don't forget your passport.
customs|go through + customs|Customs (la douane) est toujours pluriel et prend « go through » : We went through customs quickly.
souvenir|buy + a souvenir + for + person|Pour offrir : buy something for someone. I bought a souvenir for my mother.
destination|a popular + destination|Destination se combine avec popular, final ou tourist : Paris is a popular destination.
ticket|buy + a + type of + ticket|On précise le type devant ticket : a train ticket, a bus ticket. I need to buy a train ticket.
platform|from + platform + number|On dit « from platform 4 » (le quai d'où part le train). The train leaves from platform 4.
fare|the + transport + fare|Fare est le prix du trajet : the bus fare, the train fare. The bus fare is two euros.
sightseeing|go + sightseeing|« Go sightseeing » = faire du tourisme, sans article. We went sightseeing all day.
reservation|make + a reservation + for + number|On fait une réservation : make a reservation for two. Avec « make », pas « do ».
crossing|the crossing + take + duration|Crossing = traversée. Le verbe take indique la durée : The crossing takes one hour.
map|read + a + map|On lit une carte : read a map. Can you read this map?
abroad|verb + abroad|Abroad veut dire « à l'étranger » sans préposition : study abroad, live abroad. Pas « in abroad ».
commute|my + commute + take + duration|Commute est le trajet domicile-travail, aussi verbe : My commute takes forty minutes.
layover|a + duration + layover + in + place|Layover = escale longue. La durée précède le mot : a six-hour layover in Dubai.
colleague|my + colleague + verb|Colleague désigne un collègue de travail. My colleague helped me with the report.
deadline|the deadline + be + day|On dit « the deadline is Friday » sans préposition. Pour dépasser : miss the deadline.
salary|ask for + a higher + salary|On demande une augmentation : ask for a higher salary. Salary est le salaire mensuel ou annuel.
meeting|the meeting + start + at + time|À + heure : at ten. On dit « the meeting starts », « attend a meeting ».
workload|my + workload + be + adjective|Workload est indénombrable : my workload is too heavy. Pas de « s ».
manager|my + manager + be + adjective|Manager = responsable. On le décrit après « my » : My manager is very kind.
interview|have + a job interview|On dit « have an interview » (pas « make »). I have a job interview tomorrow.
resume|update + your + resume|Resume (américain) = CV. En anglais britannique : CV. Update your resume.
promotion|get + a promotion|On obtient une promotion : get a promotion. She got a promotion last month.
experience|have + duration + of experience|Experience (expérience pro) est indénombrable : five years of experience.
achievement|a big + achievement|Achievement = réussite. Souvent avec big ou great : a big achievement.
negotiate|negotiate + a + noun|Negotiate se construit directement avec l'objet : negotiate a better price.
schedule|my + schedule + be + adjective|Schedule = emploi du temps. My schedule is full this week.
self-employed|be + self-employed|Self-employed est un adjectif : he is self-employed. Pas d'article.
stakeholder|inform + stakeholders|Stakeholder = partie prenante. We must inform all stakeholders.
resign|resign + from + job|Resign = démissionner. On peut dire « resign from a job » ou simplement « resign ». He resigned last week.
apply|apply + for + a job|Apply se construit avec « for » : apply for a job. Pas « apply a job ».
budget|have + a small/large + budget|Budget se combine avec small, tight, large. We have a small budget.
feedback|thank you for + your feedback|Feedback est indénombrable : pas de « s ». Thank you for your feedback.
headache|have + a + adjective + headache|Headache = mal de tête, avec « have a ». I have a terrible headache.
fever|have + a fever|Avec fever on dit « have a fever ». The child has a fever.
prescription|need + a prescription + for + noun|On a besoin d'une ordonnance pour un médicament : a prescription for this.
symptom|a + common + symptom|Symptom = symptôme. A cough is a common symptom.
recover|recover + from + illness|Recover from se traduit par « se remettre de ». He is recovering from the flu.
injury|have + a + body part + injury|On place la partie du corps devant injury : a knee injury.
allergy|have + an allergy + to + noun|Allergy s'utilise avec « to » : an allergy to peanuts.
appointment|have + an appointment + at + time|On prend rendez-vous : make an appointment. I have a doctor's appointment at four.
exhausted|be + exhausted + after + noun|Exhausted = épuisé. Après un effort : after the trip.
stress|stress + can + verb|Stress est indénombrable : stress can affect your sleep.
healthy|healthy + noun|Healthy = sain, en bonne santé : a healthy snack, a healthy diet. Fruit is a healthy snack.
exercise|do + exercise|Avec exercise (activité physique), on dit « do exercise » ou « take exercise ». I do exercise every morning.
diet|a balanced + diet|Diet = régime alimentaire. A balanced diet is important.
dizzy|feel + dizzy|Feel + adjectif : I feel dizzy. On peut aussi dire I'm dizzy.
pain|have + a pain + in + body part|Pain = douleur. On dit « a pain in my back » avec « in ».
treatment|the treatment + last + duration|Treatment = traitement. The treatment lasts six weeks.
sore|have + a sore + body part|Sore = douloureux. I have a sore throat.
bandage|put on + a bandage|Put on = mettre. The nurse put on a bandage.
well-being|noun + be + vital for + well-being|Well-being = bien-être, indénombrable. Sleep is vital for your well-being.
checkup|go for + a checkup|Go for a checkup = aller faire un bilan de santé. I go for a checkup every year.
breakfast|have + breakfast + at + time|Pas d'article avec breakfast : have breakfast. I have breakfast at seven.
recipe|this + recipe + be + adjective|Recipe = recette. This recipe is very easy.
ingredient|the main + ingredient|Ingredient = ingrédient. Flour is the main ingredient.
delicious|noun + be + delicious|Delicious = délicieux (plus fort que good). The soup was delicious.
leftovers|eat + the leftovers|Leftovers (restes) est toujours pluriel. We ate the leftovers for lunch.
bland|be + a bit + bland|Bland = fade. On adoucit avec « a bit » : a bit bland.
spicy|love + spicy + food|Spicy = épicé, fort. I love spicy food. Attention : hot peut aussi vouloir dire chaud.
bill|can we have + the bill|Au restaurant (britannique) : the bill. En américain : the check.
waiter|the waiter + be + adjective|Waiter (serveur), waitress (serveuse). The waiter was very polite.
vegetarian|be + vegetarian|Vegetarian peut être un adjectif (she is vegetarian) ou un nom (she is a vegetarian).
fresh|fresh + noun|Fresh = frais : fresh bread, fresh fruit. I buy fresh bread every day.
crave|be craving + noun|Crave = avoir très envie. On le met souvent en -ing : I'm craving chocolate.
portion|the portions + be + adjective|Portion = part servie. The portions are huge here.
bake|bake + noun + on + day|Bake = cuire au four. I bake bread on Sundays.
chop|chop + noun + adverb|Chop = couper en morceaux. Chop the onions finely.
simmer|let + noun + simmer|Let + objet + verbe sans to : let the sauce simmer.
menu|can I see + the menu|Pour demander poliment : Can I see the menu?
snack|eat + noun + as a snack|As a snack = en guise de collation.
appetite|lose + your appetite|Lose your appetite = perdre l'appétit. I've lost my appetite.
cuisine|love + nationality + cuisine|Cuisine = cuisine d'un pays : Italian cuisine, French cuisine.
neighbour|my + neighbour + be + adjective|Neighbour (britannique), neighbor (américain). My neighbour is very friendly.
furniture|buy + new + furniture|Furniture est indénombrable : pas de « s ». We bought new furniture.
rent|the rent + be + adjective|Rent = loyer, aussi verbe (louer). The rent is too expensive.
landlord|the landlord + verb|Landlord = propriétaire qui loue. The landlord fixed the heater.
chore|noun-ing + be + my + chore|Chore = corvée. Washing dishes is my least favourite chore.
tidy|keep + noun + tidy|Keep + objet + adjectif : keep your room tidy.
cosy|the + room + be + cosy|Cosy = douillet. The living room is very cosy.
garden|have + a + adjective + garden|Garden = jardin (américain : yard). They have a big garden.
appliance|a new + kitchen appliance|Appliance = appareil ménager.
drawer|in + the drawer|Drawer = tiroir, avec « in ». The keys are in the drawer.
ceiling|the ceiling + be + adjective|Ceiling = plafond. The ceiling is very high.
leak|there is + a leak + under/in + place|Leak = fuite. There is a leak under the sink.
move house|be moving house + in + month|Move house = déménager (britannique). We are moving house in June.
household|every + household|Household = foyer, ménage. Every household received a letter.
laundry|do + the laundry|Laundry = linge, indénombrable. I do the laundry on Saturdays.
doorbell|ring + the doorbell|Doorbell = sonnette. Someone rang the doorbell.
shelf|on + the shelf|Shelf = étagère, pluriel shelves. Put the books on the shelf.
spacious|be + bright and spacious|Spacious = spacieux. The flat is bright and spacious.
renovate|be renovating + noun|Renovate = rénover. They are renovating the whole house.
utensil|noun + be + utensils|Utensil = ustensile. Knives and spoons are utensils.
worried|be + worried + about + noun|Worried se construit avec « about » : I'm worried about the exam.
embarrassed|feel + embarrassed + when + clause|Embarrassed = gêné. I felt embarrassed when I forgot his name.
excited|be + excited + about + noun|Excited se construit avec « about » : We are excited about the trip. Attention : pas « excité » au sens français négatif.
disappointed|be + disappointed + with + noun|Disappointed with something. I was disappointed with the result.
relieved|be + so relieved + clause|Relieved = soulagé. I'm so relieved it's over.
jealous|be + jealous + of + person|Jealous se construit avec « of » : jealous of her friend's success.
grateful|be + grateful + for + noun|Grateful for something. I'm grateful for your help.
lonely|feel + lonely + in + place|Lonely = seul (sentiment). He felt lonely in the new city.
annoyed|be + annoyed + with + person|Annoyed with someone, annoyed at something. I'm annoyed with him.
overwhelmed|feel + overwhelmed + by + noun|Overwhelmed by something = submergé. I feel overwhelmed by work.
proud|be + proud + of + person|Proud of someone. I'm proud of you.
nervous|be + nervous + before + noun|Nervous = nerveux, stressé. On précise avec before ou about : nervous before presentations.
thrilled|be + thrilled + to + verb|Thrilled = ravi. Suivi de « to + verbe » : We were thrilled to win.
miserable|feel + miserable + in + place|Miserable = très malheureux. He felt miserable in the rain.
confident|feel + confident + about + noun|Confident about something. She feels confident about the test.
reluctant|be + reluctant + to + verb|Reluctant = réticent, toujours suivi de « to + verbe » : reluctant to leave.
bewildered|look + bewildered + by + noun|Bewildered = perplexe. Avec look : She looked bewildered by the rules.
upset|be + upset + about + noun|Upset = contrarié. Upset about the news.
content|seem + content + with + noun|Content (adjectif) = satisfait, accent sur la 2e syllabe. Content with her life.
curious|be + naturally + curious|Curious = curieux. Children are naturally curious.
wake up|wake up + at + time|Wake up est un verbe à particule : I wake up at six. Jamais « wake up me » : wake me up.
neighbourhood|a quiet + neighbourhood|Neighbourhood = quartier (américain : neighborhood).
errand|run + errands|Run errands = faire des courses, des démarches. I have a few errands to run.
grocery|do + the grocery shopping|On dit « do the grocery shopping » ou « go grocery shopping ».
queue|a long + queue + at + place|Queue (britannique) = file d'attente. En américain : line.
habit|a bad/good + habit|Habit = habitude. Biting nails is a bad habit.

procrastinate|procrastinate + before + noun|Procrastinate = remettre à plus tard. I procrastinate before exams.

rush hour|avoid + the rush hour|Rush hour = heures de pointe. Avoid the rush hour if you can.
diary|write + in + my diary|On écrit « in my diary ». Diary = journal intime.
mess|be + a mess|Be a mess = être en désordre. My room is a mess.

miss the bus|miss + the + vehicle|Miss = rater : miss the bus, miss the train. I missed the bus this morning.

algorithm|the algorithm + suggest + noun|Algorithm = algorithme. The algorithm suggests videos.
subscribe|subscribe + to + noun|Subscribe se construit avec « to » : subscribe to a channel.

# verbs
be|subject + be + adjective/noun|Be (être) change selon le sujet : I am, you are, he/she/it is, we/they are. She was tired yesterday.
have|subject + have/has + noun|Have (avoir) devient has avec he, she, it. Au passé : had. We had a great time.
do|subject + do/does + noun|Do sert à faire une action et aide à la négation : I don't know. I did my homework.
go|go + to + place|Go se construit avec « to » + lieu : go to the beach. Sans to avec home : go home.
come|come + from/to + place|Come (venir) s'utilise avec from ou to : He came home late. Sans to avec home.
see|see + object|See = percevoir sans effort. Pour regarder volontairement, on dit look at ou watch. I saw a bird.
eat|eat + food|Eat se construit directement avec l'aliment : We ate pizza. Pas de préposition.
drink|drink + liquid|Drink + liquide : She drank a glass of water. Participe passé : drunk.
take|take + object|Take a plusieurs sens : prendre (the bus), durer (it takes an hour). He took the bus.
give|give + person + thing|Give peut s'écrire give someone something : She gave me a present.
make|make + object|Make = fabriquer, créer : make dinner, make a decision. Pour une activité : do.
get|get + object / adjective|Get = recevoir ou devenir : get a letter, get tired. Participe passé : got (UK), gotten (US).
know|know + person/fact|Know = connaître ou savoir. Pas de forme -ing en général : I know the answer.
think|think + (that) + clause|Think + that + proposition. On peut omettre « that » : I thought it was easy.
say|say + (to person) + words|Say + les mots : He said hello. Say to someone, tell someone.
tell|tell + person + information|Tell + personne : She told me a secret (pas de « to » avant la personne). Exceptions : tell the truth, tell a story, tell a lie.
write|write + noun / to person|Write + chose, ou write to + personne : He wrote me a letter.
read|read + text|Read s'écrit pareil au présent et au passé, mais se prononce différemment : « reed » au présent, « red » au passé.
buy|buy + object (+ for person)|Buy = acheter. buy something for someone. We bought a new sofa.
sit|sit + (down) + place|Sit down = s'asseoir. They sat by the window.
stand|stand + (up) + place|Stand up = se lever. She stood in the queue.
run|run + to + place|Run se construit avec to + lieu : He ran to the station. Run a business = diriger.
sleep|sleep + duration|Sleep est intransitif : The baby slept all night. Pas d'objet direct.
speak|speak + to + person|Speak to someone, speak a language. She spoke to the manager.
begin|begin + noun / to + verb|Begin ou start. The film began at eight. Begin to + verbe.
bring|bring + object + (to place)|Bring = apporter vers ici. He brought his umbrella.
find|find + object|Find = trouver. I found my keys under the bed. Find out = découvrir.
feel|feel + adjective|Feel + adjectif : I felt happy. Feel like + nom ou -ing.
keep|keep + object / -ing|Keep + objet = garder. Keep + -ing = continuer. She kept the receipt.
leave|leave + place|Leave + lieu = quitter : We left early. Leave for = partir pour.
lose|lose + object|Lose = perdre. He lost his wallet. Ne pas confondre avec loose (lâche).
meet|meet + person|Meet se construit sans préposition : I met her at a party.
pay|pay + (for) + noun|Pay for something, pay someone. He paid by card.
send|send + person + thing|Send someone something ou send something to someone. She sent me a message.
spend|spend + time/money + (on / -ing)|Spend s'utilise pour le temps et l'argent : We spent a week in Spain.
teach|teach + person + subject|Teach someone something. She taught English for ten years.
wear|wear + clothing|Wear = porter un vêtement. He wore a blue suit. Pour carry, on porte un objet.
win|win + noun|Win se construit avec l'objet gagné : win a match, win a prize. Our team won the match.
fall|fall + off/down + noun|Fall off = tomber de. He fell off his bike.
fly|fly + to + place|Fly = voler, aller en avion : We flew to Rome.
drive|drive + vehicle / person|Drive a car. Drive someone to a place : She drove us to the airport.
ride|ride + bike/horse|Ride se dit pour le vélo, le cheval : He rode his bike to school.
swim|swim + place|Swim in the lake, swim across the river. We swam in the lake.
sing|sing + song|Sing a song. She sang a beautiful song.
cut|cut + object|Cut est identique à tous les temps : cut, cut, cut. I cut my finger.
put|put + object + place|Put + objet + lieu : She put the keys on the table. Identique à tous les temps.
let|let + person + verb (no to)|Let + objet + verbe nu : Her parents let her go out.
set|set + object|Set the table, set an alarm. Identique à tous les temps.
break|break + object|Break = casser. The child broke a window. Participe passé : broken.
build|build + object|Build = construire. They built a bridge.
catch|catch + object|Catch = attraper. He caught the ball. Catch a bus = prendre le bus.
choose|choose + object|Choose = choisir. She chose the red dress.
draw|draw + object|Draw = dessiner. He drew a house.
forget|forget + object / to + verb|Forget + objet, ou forget to + verbe. I forgot her number.
grow|grow + noun / adjective|Grow = grandir. Grow up = devenir adulte. The kids grew quickly.
hear|hear + object|Hear = entendre sans effort. Pour écouter volontairement : listen to. I heard a strange noise.
hide|hide + object|Hide = cacher. She hid the gift.
hold|hold + object|Hold = tenir. He held her hand.
lend|lend + person + object|Lend = prêter (je donne). Borrow = emprunter. I lent him my book.
mean|mean + object / clause|What do you mean? Mean = vouloir dire. What did she mean?
ring|ring + object|Ring the bell. Le téléphone aussi : The phone rang twice.
rise|rise + place|Rise est intransitif : The sun rose at six. Différent de raise (lever quelque chose).
sell|sell + object (+ to person)|Sell = vendre. They sold their old car.
shake|shake + object|Shake = secouer. He shook my hand.
show|show + person + object|Show someone something. She showed me the photos.
steal|steal + object|Steal = voler. Someone stole my bike. Rob s'emploie pour un lieu ou une personne.
throw|throw + object (+ adverb)|Throw = lancer. He threw the ball far.
understand|understand + object|Understand = comprendre. I understood the lesson.
wake|wake + (up)|Wake up = se réveiller. She woke early today. Wake someone up = réveiller.
cost|cost + amount|Cost = coûter. The ticket cost ten euros. Identique à tous les temps.
learn|learn + object / to + verb|Learn + objet, ou learn to + verbe. I learned a lot.
overcome|overcome + difficulty|Overcome = surmonter. She overcame her fear. Participe passé : overcome.
undertake|undertake + task|Undertake = entreprendre. They undertook a big project.

# expressions
a piece of cake|subject + be + a piece of cake|Expression figée : on ne change pas les mots. Elle s'emploie après « be » pour dire que c'est facile. The exam was a piece of cake.
break a leg|Break a leg!|Interjection figée, dite avant un spectacle. Ne se dit pas au sens propre. Break a leg tonight!
under the weather|be/feel + under the weather|S'emploie après be ou feel pour dire qu'on est un peu malade. I'm feeling under the weather today.
once in a blue moon|subject + verb + once in a blue moon|S'ajoute en fin de phrase pour dire « très rarement ». We go to the cinema once in a blue moon.
the ball is in your court|the ball + be + in + possessive + court|On change le possessif : your, his, her court. I made my offer; now the ball is in your court.

hit the nail on the head|hit the nail on the head|Verbe conjugué : you hit, she hit. Le mot « nail » reste au singulier. You hit the nail on the head.
cost an arm and a leg|subject + cost + an arm and a leg|Cost est irrégulier (cost, cost, cost). Expression familière. That car cost an arm and a leg.
let the cat out of the bag|let the cat out of the bag (about + noun)|Let est identique à tous les temps. On révèle un secret par erreur. He let the cat out of the bag about the surprise.

bite the bullet|bite the bullet|Verbe conjugué : bit the bullet. Souvent avec « had to ». I had to bite the bullet and call him.

burn the midnight oil|burn the midnight oil|Verbe conjugué : burned (ou burnt). She burned the midnight oil before the exam.
